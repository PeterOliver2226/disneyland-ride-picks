The ratings

    How “Best for both kids” ranks

    Each kid's rating gets points: Highly 3, Medium 2, Somewhat 1, any skip or can't-ride 0. The score is the average of the two kids, so a ride both will enjoy beats one that only suits the 4-year-old. Ties go to the ride with the higher single rating. Starred rides always come first.

    Heights and taking turns

    The baby can't ride anything with a minimum height. Rider Switch lets one adult wait with the kids while the other rides, then swap without waiting again.
